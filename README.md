# One Match. One Galaxy.

A make.fun bounty entry for **ai-excited-01** — "Make the AI judge excited".

A single wooden matchstick runs diagonally across a pure black background.
Its tip is lit — and inside the flame burns a huge spiral galaxy: blue,
violet and gold stars swirling around a bright white-gold core, edged with
a thin rim of orange fire. One match. One galaxy.
